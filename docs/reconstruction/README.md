## 🏗️ Overview

Recover scene geometry from images, align local predictions and scale reconstruction to longer sequences or larger scenes. Outputs may include depth, pointmaps and camera estimates. Full SfM and SLAM systems that jointly recover scene structure and trajectories belong here. Reusable acceleration, compression, adaptation and reliability interventions belong in [Model Extensions](../extensions/). Check each method for its camera assumptions and post-processing.

**Reading tags:** static geometry · streaming · mixed cameras · SLAM.

These tags are reading cues; the generated list below records the primary folder. Notes may contain Korean.
