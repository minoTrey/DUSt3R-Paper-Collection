## Overview

Methods that change the cost, behavior, reliability or pretraining of the geometry models in this collection. The core reading route remains [CroCo, CroCo v2, DUSt3R, MASt3R and VGGT](../foundation).

The generated list below has three reading groups. **Efficiency & compression** covers quantization, token/attention reduction, compact students and cache management. **Adaptation & reliability** covers new sensing/camera conditions, coordinate frames, resolution and uncertainty. **Further pretraining** covers reusable self-supervised, weakly supervised and training-recipe contributions, including Muskie, GEKKO, VLRC, E-RayZer, Reliev3R and CARVE, separately from the five core starting models.

Training requirements, input calibration and inference-time optimization vary by method. A faster or smaller model can trade accuracy for cost; use each note's source-specific protocol and limitations.

[Legacy-to-current paper and card paths](../../data/paper-path-migrations.json) support existing card-production jobs. No category move stubs are retained; historical mappings and PNG aliases are excluded from paper counts.
